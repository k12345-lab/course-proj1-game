# Product Thinking Resources

## Knowledge

- [IDG: Product Thinking learning pathway (hub)](https://www.idg.gov.sg/product-thinking/)
  Primary curriculum. There are 7 modules plus 3 learning objectives: 4Cs, outputs vs outcomes, and Five Whys. Use for: the overall sequence, and when citing IDG's framing.
- [IDG Module 1: Understanding the Problem](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking1/)
  "Solutions in disguise", the three principles, and "nail one problem first". Use for: the problem-vs-solution mindset.
- [IDG Module 3 (URL pt2): Start With The Whys](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking2/)
  Five Whys, with red-light and grant-form examples, and "choosing the right level to fix". Use for: root cause analysis.
- [IDG Module 2 (URL pt3): Craft a Clear Problem Statement](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking3/)
  The 4Cs, worked on the repeated form-filling example. Use for: writing problem statements.
- [IDG Module 4: Metrics](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking4/)
  SMART, leading and lagging indicators, and the Value-Cost Ratio. Use for: choosing metrics.
- [IDG Module 5: Assumptions and Risks](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking5/)
  Market, technical and team risk, the 4 de-risking stages, and the health-booking A/B test. Use for: de-risking.
- [IDG Module 6: A Good Customer Experience](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking6/)
  Design principles, ParkingSG, Redeem.sg, and the 11-star framework. Use for: customer experience.
- [IDG Module 7: Key Takeaways](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking7/)
  The toolkit summary and the three shifts. Use for: review and spaced retrieval.
- `sources/idg-notes.md` (local)
  Verbatim transcription of every module, including the infographics that hold most of the content. Use for: exact quotes when authoring lessons.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [ProductTank Singapore (Mind the Product meetup)](https://www.meetup.com/ProductTank-Singapore/)
  Free, volunteer-run meetups open to anyone in product. Use for: hearing how working PMs frame problems and pick metrics.
- [Mind the Product: ProductTank overview](https://www.mindtheproduct.com/producttank/)
  The same meetup format runs in many cities. Use for: finding a local group if not in Singapore.
