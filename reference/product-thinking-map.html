Three principles (IDG Module 1)

  1. Focus on the problem, not the solution. covered
  2. Chase outcomes, not outputs. “A website with five buttons is an output. More citizens completing transactions is an outcome.”
  3. Integrate policy, ops, and tech. “Bring them into one room with shared goals.”

Spotting a solution in disguise covered · L1

Solution in disguise

Names a technology, product or feature: “we need AI / an app / a CRM / a dashboard”.

Problem

Names who is affected, what they're trying to do, and what's in the way.

The three questions: Who are our users? · What are they trying to do? · What's getting in their way?

Your reframing is a guess. Check it with real users before you build anything.
